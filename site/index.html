Cards for Home Assistant

A home that looks good on every screen.

Lumen is a set of cards for your Home Assistant dashboard: lights, alarm, air conditioning, weather, people and more. You set them up in the regular edit window, without writing code.

Get started

Three steps to your first card

You need Home Assistant 2026.6 or newer and the HACS add-on store.

  1. Install with HACS

    1. Open HACS, then the ⋮ menu → Custom repositories.
    2. Paste https://github.com/dkej123/lumen-cards, type Dashboard, Add.
    3. Search for Lumen Cards and click Download.
    Without HACS

    Copy lumen-cards.js and the font files to config/www/lumen-cards/. Then in Settings → Dashboards → ⋮ → Resources, add /local/lumen-cards/lumen-cards.js as a JavaScript module.

  2. Refresh your browser

    On a computer, press Ctrl+F5 (on a Mac, Cmd+Shift+R).

    In the phone app: Settings → Companion app → Debugging → Reset frontend cache.

  3. Add a card

    Click the pencil on your dashboard, then + Add card, and type Lumen. Pick a card and your device, and the preview changes right away.

    On the By entity tab, Lumen suggests a card for the device you pick.

Questions

When something does not work

I installed it, but I do not see the cards.

Refresh your browser with Ctrl+F5 (on a Mac, Cmd+Shift+R). In the phone app: Settings → Companion app → Debugging → Reset frontend cache. Then type “Lumen” when adding a card.

I see “Custom element doesn't exist: lumen-…”.

The same fix: refresh your browser. If you still see it, check Settings → Dashboards → ⋮ → Resources. There should be one entry ending in lumen-cards.js.

Cards look different on a phone and on a tablet.

That is intended: a small card shows its compact version. In the card's settings you can choose the Full or Compact look so it does not switch.

How do I get a background with the real weather?

Add the Lumen · Weather background card once per view and pick your forecast in it. The sky behind the cards shows the sun, clouds, rain or snow, by day and by night.

A floating button covers something.

Several floating buttons stack above each other by themselves. To lift them higher, for example above a bar from another add-on, add lumen-cards-fab-bottom: 90px to your Home Assistant theme.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Cards have an accent colour in their settings, and cards with lights also a glow colour. To change the glow for all cards at once, set lumen-cards-glow-color in your Home Assistant theme.

Can I use the cards with a keyboard?

Yes. Tab moves between cards, Enter or Space is a tap, and Shift+Enter is a hold. Arrow keys change the brightness, and Escape closes windows.
